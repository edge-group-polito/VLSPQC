## (a) Paragraph

We benchmarked the level-1 variants of FAEST, MAYO, MQOM, QR-UOV, SDitH, SNOVA, SQIsign and UOV (39 variants) using the reference C code of each package, compiled with plain gcc \texttt{-O2}, with one operation per process, pinned to one core, and a 1000-byte message. Table~\ref{tab:l1} reports the per-metric median of three runs. FAEST has the smallest public key of all variants (32 B) and the smallest key material overall (pk+sk of 64 B); only MAYO has a smaller secret key (24 B). Its signatures are large: 3906 to 5924 B, against 2492 to 3316 B for MQOM, 3721 to 4914 B for SDitH, and at most 541 B for MAYO, QR-UOV, SNOVA, SQIsign and UOV. Key generation takes about 0.002 ms, more than ten times less than any other scheme (next: SDitH, 0.0269 ms). Signing and verification are slower: the fastest variant, FAEST-EM-128f, needs 10.8 ms and 7.56 ms, i.e. 2.1 to 10.6 times (sign) and 2.1 to 9.3 times (verify) slower than the best MQOM, QR-UOV, SNOVA and MAYO variants, but 2.1 and 2.8 times faster than the best SDitH variant. Signing with FAEST needs between 354.1 and 2985.7 KiB of heap plus stack, comparable to MQOM and SDitH.

## (c) Discussion (Section III-A style)

FAEST stands out for its keys: a 32-byte public key and a 32-byte secret key are the smallest pk+sk of all level-1 variants, and its key generation, at about 0.002 ms, is more than ten times faster than that of the next scheme. The price is paid at signing time and in the signature: FAEST-128f has the largest signature of all variants (5924 B), and the best SNOVA, QR-UOV, MAYO and UOV variants sign 2.6, 3.6, 10.6 and 46 times faster, respectively, than the best FAEST variant. In our runs FAEST signs and verifies faster than the best SDitH variant (2.1 and 2.8 times), while for SQIsign the best FAEST signing time is 3.6 times lower and the verification times differ by 1.47 times, which is within the run-to-run spread, so we make no claim for that case. The EM variants are faster and smaller than the corresponding 128f/128s variants (for example sign 10.8 ms vs. 14.6 ms, signature 5060 B vs. 5924 B); the s variants have smaller signatures but need 2464.3 and 2981.7 KiB (EM-128s, 128s) of heap when signing. UOV-Is-pkc shows the opposite trade-off to FAEST: a 96 B signature, but a 66576 B public key.

## (d) Methodology and caveats (to be placed in the setup paragraph / footnote)

All schemes are measured with their reference, non-optimised, portable C implementations (no AVX2, AES-NI or other platform-specific paths, no external crypto library), gcc 13.3 \texttt{-O2}, on an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H under WSL2 (hybrid P/E-core CPU), with each operation in its own process pinned to one logical CPU. The time of an operation is the median over up to 1000 calls (at most 3 s budget, at least 5 calls) of a 1000-byte message. Absolute numbers are therefore not comparable with those of papers that benchmark optimised packages, which are faster, and cycle counts are raw TSC ticks, relative only under WSL2. We repeated the whole benchmark three times, the machine not being perfectly quiet (the first run shared the machine with about one busy core of editor background load); the median relative difference between two runs of the same (variant, operation) pair is 9.0\% to 26.7\% (maximum 111.6\%), and the per-metric (max-min)/median spread over the three runs has a median of 24.7\% to 28.2\% over variants. Differences between schemes below about 1.5 times are therefore not claimed. Heap, stack and code size were identical in all three runs.

Correctness checks and provenance of the ported sources (to be summarised in a footnote or the artefact description):
- Sizes agree with the official known-answer tests (KAT, record 0) for MAYO, SNOVA, SDitH, SQIsign and MQOM (all 18 MQOM variants, record 0 only); the MAYO, SNOVA and SDitH ports matched the official KAT on 10 records, SQIsign on 2 to 3 records.
- UOV and QR-UOV (16 of the 39 variants) have no official KAT available offline; they were checked against each package's own generator on records 0 and 1 only.
- FAEST sizes were not compared with an official KAT in this run; the FAEST tree carries one KAT vector per variant.
- The MQOM package directories were empty; the sources come from a complete earlier copy of unknown archive origin, whose record 0 matches the official KAT.
- SQIsign (static build) needs \texttt{-I.} and SDitH needs \texttt{-lm}.
- Memory figures are heap (allocator wrapper) and stack (page granularity, 4 KiB) of the scheme; the harness's own key and message buffers are not charged, so UOV and QR-UOV figures exclude key storage. Stack values of 4 KiB and below are floors, not measurements. VmHWM (in `results.csv`) is indicative only and is not used in the text.
- Not all medians are from the same run: each metric of each row is the median over the three runs separately.
